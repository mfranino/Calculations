Engineering assumptions

Uniform elastic uniaxial shaft stress, isotropic material, correctly wired four-active-gauge full bridge with axial and transverse gauges at diametrically opposite locations. Ideal bridge response: |Vout/Vexc| ≈ GF(1+ν)|ε|/2. The sign depends on bridge wiring.

Shunt calibration verifies electrical response, not mechanical force sensitivity. For turbine use, verify mechanical calibration, bending/torsion rejection, temperature compensation, telemetry bandwidth and time synchronization. Axial load at the instrumented shaft section is not necessarily identical to hydraulic runner thrust.
